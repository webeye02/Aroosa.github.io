Turnaround Time (TAT) Dashboard

Tracks how many days a rating takes to move from initiation through Rating Committee (RC), PPL and NL, so slow stages are easy to spot.

  • Average days for each stage, plus the total TAT
  • Slicers for sector, rating action, opinion, rating type, analyst and team lead
  • Filter by dissemination date
  • Linked to the rest of the report through page navigation
